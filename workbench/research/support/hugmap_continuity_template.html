Hugmap: continuous contact branches

Each roll has a separate function: f(x,y,D) = minimum required friction over valid contact roots. The coloured map is f < 0.5. This viewer compares posture changes and friction along selected straight paths. It does not prove dynamics or global continuity.

Blue: minimum-friction solution. Orange: sampled path minimizing its largest joint step, constrained to μ < 0.5. Dashed: friction cutoff. Blank intervals mean no root in the catalog, not certified impossibility. A changing winning contact branch can make the blue posture jump even if another continuous path exists.

Functions are built from CAD trigonometric geometry and polynomial roots, not fitted to the JSON. Added contact pairs repair sampled catalog gaps; catalog coverage remains incomplete. Roll changes and approach/holding dynamics are outside this audit.
