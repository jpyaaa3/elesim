Two bands, one geometric relation

The short equations

Use millimetres, R=D/2, X=(roll/90°)x, Y=y−119.5, A=R+35, κ=25 cot(q3/10).
(X+κ)² + Y² ≈ (κ+A)²
Therefore q3 ≈ 10 atan[50(X−A)/(A²−X²−Y²)] (radians). q3 and q4 are total bends of the five-node segments; controls and the q plot use degrees.

Band 1 crosses q3=0 at |x| = D/2 + 35 mm. C/S is a sign change along this family, not automatically a separate grasp family.

The second condition is contact with the distal rail. Intersect its R-offset line with the first circle: only trigonometry and a square root. Rounded endpoints use two-circle intersection. On the side-contact branch, with signed circle radius a and rail distance d, μ²=(1+d/a)/(1−d/a); μ<0.5 means d/a<−0.6.

Limits: The contact model is approximate. Preparation uses conservative per-part convex XY envelopes for a stationary tall vertical cylinder and fixed Go2 legs; an envelope overlap can reject a feasible alternative.
