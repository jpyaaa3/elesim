# Gait-phase Preview — Final Result Notes

- Analysis window: full trial duration; visibility from sim eye-camera in-frame projection.
- Gait-phase preview achieved the lowest v-axis image-space RMS error among all tested conditions.
- Compared with uncompensated walking, v RMS decreased from 0.374 to 0.138, corresponding to approximately 63.2% reduction.
- Compared with UV feedback, gait-phase preview reduced v RMS from 0.211 to 0.138, corresponding to approximately 34.9% reduction.
- Gait-phase preview reduced vertical image-space error versus UV feedback and gaze-off baselines in this protocol (standoff 0.85 m, 30 s max duration).
